How it works
Open: the port replies SYN-ACK. Closed: it replies RST. Filtered: no reply, because a firewall dropped the packet. A SYN scan answers with RST instead of finishing the handshake, so it is faster and quieter. A connect scan completes the handshake and is easier to log. Only scan systems you own or have written permission to test.
